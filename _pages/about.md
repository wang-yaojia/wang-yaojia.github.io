I am **Yaojia Wang** (王瑶珈), a Ph.D. student in **Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering** at **Fudan University**. I received my B.Eng. in Microelectronics Science and Engineering from Fudan University in June 2026. I was also an exchange student in Electrical Engineering at the **University of California, Santa Barbara** in Fall 2024.

My work is centered on electronic design automation, FPGA design automation, and optimization for digital systems. I am especially interested in FPGA mapping, digital integrated-circuit design, graph algorithms, and resource-constrained optimization.

This site collects my CV, research experience, projects, student activities and outreach, and selected technical work.


## Highlights

- Ph.D. student at Fudan University, Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering, September 2026–present.
- B.Eng. in Microelectronics Science and Engineering, Fudan University, 2026.
- First-author work on FPGA mapping selected as an IWLS 2026 Best Student Paper Candidate.
- First-author paper on LEO satellite-network routing published at IEEE/CIC ICCC 2025 Workshops.
- Project experience in LLVM IR-based high-level synthesis, a course-based RISC-V CPU design, and satellite-network digital twins.
