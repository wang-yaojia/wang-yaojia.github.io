My research interests are mainly in EDA, FPGA design automation, and optimization methods for digital systems.

## Research Interests

- Electronic design automation for FPGA and digital systems
- FPGA mapping, logic synthesis, and resource-aware implementation
- Graph algorithms and optimization methods for computing and communication systems

## Selected Research

**A Frontier-Based Framework for Joint Covering and Pairing of Dual-Output LUTs in FPGA Mapping**  
2025.09 - 2026.04

First-author work submitted to the [International Workshop on Logic & Synthesis (IWLS) 2026](https://www.iwls.org/iwls2026/), selected as a Best Student Paper Candidate, with ICCAD 2026 under review.

- Proposed a frontier-level optimization framework for dual-output LUT mapping to improve FPGA resource utilization.
- Built a Yosys/ABC-based implementation and automated benchmark evaluation flow.
- On 20 EPFL benchmarks, reduced total LUT count from 112,050 in the ABC baseline to 70,964, a 36.67% reduction; further improved LUT count by 2.97% over a best-cut/post-packing baseline.

**Stabilizing and Optimizing Inter-Shell Routing in LEO Networks with Integrated Routing Cost**  
2024.12 - 2025.04

First-author work published at [IEEE/CIC ICCC 2025 Workshops](https://iccc2025.ieee-iccc.org/).

- Led model design and manuscript writing.
- Designed an integrated routing-cost model for multi-layer satellite networks, balancing path efficiency and link stability.
- Built simulation experiments for MEO/LEO network scenarios and compared the method with shortest-path and adaptive routing baselines.
- Reduced switching rates by 39.1% and 22.0% in comparative experiments.
